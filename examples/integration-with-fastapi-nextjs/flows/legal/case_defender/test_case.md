# Smith v. Johnson - Breach of Contract Case

## Case Details
- **Case Number**: 2024-BC-12345
- **Court**: Commercial Court of Paris
- **Filing Date**: June 10, 2024
- **Plaintiff**: Smith Consulting, Inc.
- **Defendant**: Johnson Technologies SARL
- **Jurisdiction**: France
- **Governing Law**: French Commercial Code, French Civil Code

## Nature of Dispute
Smith Consulting, Inc. has filed a lawsuit against our client, Johnson Technologies SARL, alleging breach of contract regarding IT consulting services. The plaintiff claims that Johnson failed to deliver agreed-upon consulting services worth €50,000 as specified in the contract dated January 15, 2024.

## Plaintiff's Claims
1. Johnson Technologies failed to complete the contracted IT infrastructure upgrade by the May 1, 2024 deadline
2. The partially completed work contains significant defects requiring remediation
3. Smith has suffered business losses due to the incomplete implementation
4. Smith demands €75,000 in damages (€50,000 for contract value plus €25,000 for consequential damages)

## Our Client's Position
Johnson Technologies maintains that:
1. All services were delivered according to the contract specifications by April 28, 2024
2. Smith's staff failed to provide necessary access and information required for implementation
3. The alleged defects were not part of the original scope of work
4. Smith's claimed business losses are speculative and unsubstantiated

## Evidence in Our Possession
1. Signed contract dated January 15, 2024
2. Email correspondence showing Smith's delays in providing system access
3. Project completion report dated April 28, 2024
4. Acceptance criteria document signed by both parties
5. Change request forms showing scope modifications

## Relevant Contract Provisions
- Section 3.2: "Client shall provide necessary access and information within 3 business days of request"
- Section 5.1: "Completion deadline is contingent upon client's timely provision of required resources"
- Section 7.4: "Any defects must be reported within 5 business days of delivery"
- Section 9.2: "Consequential damages are limited to direct and proven losses"

## Applicable Laws
- Article L. 441-6 of the French Commercial Code regarding payment terms
- Articles 1231-1 and 1231-2 of the French Civil Code regarding contractual liability
- Article 1103 of the French Civil Code regarding the binding force of contracts
