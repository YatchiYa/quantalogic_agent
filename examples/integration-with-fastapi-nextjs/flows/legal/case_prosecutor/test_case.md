# State v. Thompson - Financial Fraud Case

## Case Details
- **Case Number**: 2024-FF-78932
- **Court**: Criminal Court of Paris
- **Filing Date**: June 5, 2024
- **Plaintiff**: The State
- **Defendant**: James Thompson
- **Jurisdiction**: France
- **Governing Law**: French Penal Code, French Financial Code

## Nature of Case
The State is investigating James Thompson, Finance Director at TechCorp SA, for alleged embezzlement of company funds totaling €120,000 between January and April 2024.

## Evidence Available
1. Financial records showing irregular transfers from company accounts to Thompson's personal account:
   - January 15, 2024: €25,000
   - February 12, 2024: €35,000
   - March 18, 2024: €30,000
   - April 22, 2024: €30,000

2. Internal audit report dated May 15, 2024, documenting the discovery of unauthorized transfers

3. System access logs showing Thompson accessed the financial system on the dates of all transfers

4. Thompson's employment contract and job description specifying authorization limits of €5,000 without secondary approval

5. Testimony from CFO stating no bonuses were authorized during this period

6. Thompson's bank records showing unusual spending patterns following each transfer

7. Email from Thompson to accounting dated January 10, 2024, asking about "discrete fund transfer procedures"

## Witness Information
1. Marie Dubois - CFO of TechCorp SA
   - Can testify about authorization procedures and lack of bonus approvals
   - Has worked with Thompson for 5 years

2. Ahmed Benali - Internal Auditor
   - Discovered the discrepancies during routine audit
   - Can explain the company's financial controls

3. Sophie Martin - Accounting Clerk
   - Received unusual instructions from Thompson regarding transfers
   - Was told these were "confidential executive bonuses"

4. Jean Moreau - CEO of TechCorp SA
   - Can confirm no executive bonuses were approved
   - Was alerted by CFO about the suspicious transfers

## Defendant Background
- James Thompson, 42 years old
- Finance Director at TechCorp SA for 8 years
- No prior criminal record
- Previously worked at three other companies without incident
- Currently on administrative leave from TechCorp SA

## Applicable Laws
- Article 314-1 of the French Penal Code (Breach of Trust)
- Article 314-2 of the French Penal Code (Aggravated Breach of Trust)
- Article L. 241-3 of the French Commercial Code (Misuse of Corporate Assets)
- Article 121-3 of the French Penal Code (Intent Requirements)
